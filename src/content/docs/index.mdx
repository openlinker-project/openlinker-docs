## What is OpenLinker?

OpenLinker is an open-source, self-hosted e-commerce orchestration platform from the
[openlinker-project](https://github.com/openlinker-project). It keeps orders, inventory and
listings in sync between your own shop and the marketplaces you sell on — and connects the
shipping and invoicing systems around them — without handing your data to a SaaS channel manager.

Shops (PrestaShop, WooCommerce), marketplaces (Allegro, ERLI), couriers (InPost, DPD) and
invoicing (KSeF, Subiekt nexo) plug in as adapters behind typed capability ports, so a new
platform is an adapter built against a stable SDK. OpenLinker is licensed under Apache 2.0 and
runs anywhere Docker does.

- Learn what it does and who it is for on the [OpenLinker website](https://openlinker.io/en/).
- Click through a shared, view-only instance in the [OpenLinker live demo](https://app.demo.openlinker.io/).
- Read and contribute to the code in the [OpenLinker GitHub repository](https://github.com/openlinker-project/openlinker).
